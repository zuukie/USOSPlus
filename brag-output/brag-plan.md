# Brag Plan: USOS++

## What is this app?
A high-performance, modern redesign of the legacy USOSweb university system, consolidating grades, schedules, and payments into a single fast panel.

## The angle
The "Academic OS" upgrade. We treat the transition from a 2005-era web portal to a modern dashboard as a major infrastructure milestone. Played completely straight.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Text slams in on a dark background: "University portals were built for records."
Pause.
"Not for students."

## Key moments (the middle)
- **The Flip:** A split-second flash of the legacy USOSweb UI, followed by a simulated click of the "Enable USOS++" toggle, and the modern dashboard slamming into place.
- **The Metrics:** A sequential pop-in of the "Średnia ocen" (GPA), "Punkty ECTS", and "Kolejne zajęcia" (Next Class) cards.
- **The Flow:** A smooth, cinematic pan across the weekly schedule, highlighting the clean, color-coded blocks.

## Outro / punchline
Logo "USOS++" slams in center screen.
Tagline: "University, upgraded."
Small URL: `chromewebstore.google.com/detail/usos++`

## User flow worth showing
Legacy USOSweb Page $\rightarrow$ Toggle Activation $\rightarrow$ Modern Dashboard Reveal.

## Tone
- Preset: yc-parody
- Creative direction: Fake Series A launch for student infrastructure.
- Interpretation: Serious, matter-of-fact delivery. No winking. The absurdity comes from the contrast between the "enterprise" tone and the fact that it's a university portal.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm bed with professional accents.
- Music: happy-beats-business-moves-vol-11-by-ende-dot-app.mp3
- Music treatment: Starts at 0s, steady volume (0.35). Subtle swell during the "Flip" reveal.
- Music cue guidance: Use strong cues at 1.60s (Hook), 3.70s (Reveal), and 17.91s (Outro).
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; use music RMS to make the dashboard cards "breathe" slightly.
- SFX posture: moderate; motion-matched.
- Audio-coupled moments: "Toggle" click $\rightarrow$ `interface/click_001`; Card pop-ins $\rightarrow$ `interface/drop_002` sequence; Outro slam $\rightarrow$ `impact/impactBell_heavy_000`.
- Restraint rule: No "magic" sound effects; keep it grounded in UI interactions.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — The Hook — 3s
Background is deep dark. Text "University portals were built for records." appears at 0.5s.
At 1.6s (Strong Cue), text changes to "Not for students."
Sequential/interaction: Text swap.
Audio intent: Establishing the problem.
Audio-coupled idea: Subtle bass hit on the text swap.
Music: Upbeat corporate bed.
Transition mood: hard cut $\rightarrow$ Scene 2

### Scene 2 — The Reveal — 5s
Flash of the legacy USOSweb (white/blue, dated). A cursor appears and clicks a "Toggle USOS++" button.
At 3.7s (Strong Cue), the screen "slams" into the USOS++ Dashboard.
Sequential/interaction: Simulated click $\rightarrow$ Layout transform.
Audio intent: The "Aha!" moment.
Audio-coupled idea: `interface/click_001` on the toggle, followed by a low-end swell.
Music: Bed continues, volume increases slightly.
Transition mood: clean slide $\rightarrow$ Scene 3

### Scene 3 — The Metrics — 5s
Focus on the Dashboard top row.
Cards pop in one by one: "Średnia ocen" $\rightarrow$ "Punkty ECTS" $\rightarrow$ "Kolejne zajęcia".
Sequential/interaction: 3 cards arrive one by one.
Audio intent: Validating the value.
Audio-coupled idea: `interface/drop_002` for each card arrival.
Music: Bed continues.
Transition mood: soft crossfade $\rightarrow$ Scene 4

### Scene 4 — The Plan — 4s
A cinematic pan over the "Plan zajęć" (Weekly Schedule).
The view scrolls vertically, showing the color-coded classes.
Sequential/interaction: Smooth scroll.
Audio intent: Demonstrating utility.
Audio-coupled idea: Subtle "whoosh" or paper-slide sound.
Music: Bed continues.
Transition mood: hard cut $\rightarrow$ Scene 5

### Scene 5 — The Outro — 3s
Background returns to deep dark.
Logo "USOS++" slams in at 17.91s (Strong Cue).
Tagline "University, upgraded." fades in below.
Sequential/interaction: Logo slam $\rightarrow$ Tagline fade.
Audio intent: Finality and brand.
Audio-coupled idea: `impact/impactBell_heavy_000` on the logo slam.
Music: Final beat $\rightarrow$ fade out.
Transition mood: none
